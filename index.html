Frequently asked questions

What is Mouse&Cheese?

Mouse&Cheese is a Discord game bot with mini-games, battles and friendly competition.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Invite Mouse&Cheese to your server and play together with your community.

How do I add the bot?

Use the Add to Discord button at the top of the page to invite the bot to your server.

Is this bot safe?

Mouse&Cheese is designed to be safe. When adding it, review the permissions Discord requests and grant only the permissions you’re comfortable with.

Can I add the bot to my Discord apps?

Yes. Open the bot’s profile and click the “Add App” button.
